Business Intelligence Rules

Generate realistic relationships based on these assumptions:

Zara launches new collections every 4–6 weeks with minimal discounts.
H&M has high promotion frequency targeting Gen Z.
Uniqlo emphasizes functional basics with premium fabrics.
Reliance Trends and Max Fashion dominate value-priced segments in Tier-2 cities.
Lifestyle and Shoppers Stop focus on premium brands and loyalty-driven campaigns.
Sustainable Fashion, Athleisure, and Smart Casual are high-growth categories.
Diwali, Wedding Season, and Independence Day significantly increase demand for ethnic wear and occasion wear.
Social media engagement strongly influences trend adoption and product sales.
Products with higher ratings should have lower return rates and higher sell-through.
Tier-2 cities respond more strongly to promotions than metro cities.
